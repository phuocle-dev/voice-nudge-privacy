VoiceNudge

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

NexusCraft Studio ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how VoiceNudge ("the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application on iOS and Android devices.

By using VoiceNudge, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

VoiceNudge is designed to work primarily on your device. You do not need to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address to use the App.

Depending on how you use the App and its integrated services, the following information may be processed.

1.1 Voice Recordings and Imported Audio

When you choose to record a voice reminder or import an audio file:

  • The App accesses your microphone when you initiate a recording.
  • The App processes audio files that you choose to import.
  • Voice recordings and imported audio files are stored locally on your device.
  • Audio files may be processed or converted into a supported format for reminder playback.
  • We do not upload your voice recordings or imported audio files to our servers or Firebase Analytics.

1.2 Reminder Information

The App may store information that you provide when creating and managing reminders, including:

  • Reminder titles and descriptions.
  • Scheduled dates and times.
  • Repeat settings and reminder schedules.
  • Associated voice recordings and sound files.
  • Reminder preferences and settings.

This information is stored locally on your device and is used to provide the App's reminder functionality.

1.3 Technical and Usage Information

VoiceNudge uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App and to improve its functionality and user experience.

Firebase Analytics may collect certain technical and usage information, including:

  • App interaction events, such as creating, deleting, enabling, and disabling reminders.
  • Screen views and app usage events.
  • App opening and session-related events, where supported.
  • App version and operating system information.
  • Device information, such as device model and language.
  • App instance identifiers and other technical identifiers used by Firebase Analytics.

Some technical and usage information may be collected automatically by Firebase Analytics, depending on the SDK configuration and device platform.

We use this information for analytics, understanding app usage, and improving the App.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by VoiceNudge is used to:

  • Create and manage voice reminders.
  • Record, import, process, and play reminder audio.
  • Schedule and deliver local notifications and alarms.
  • Remember reminder settings and preferences.
  • Understand how users interact with the App.
  • Analyze app usage and improve functionality, stability, and user experience.

We do not use voice recordings or reminder content for advertising or behavioral profiling.

3. Microphone Permission

VoiceNudge requests microphone access when you choose to record a voice reminder.

Microphone access is used only to record audio that you explicitly choose to create.

You can manage or revoke microphone permission through your device settings. If permission is denied, voice recording functionality may not be available.

4. Notifications and Alarm Permissions

VoiceNudge uses notification and supported alarm functionality to deliver reminders at scheduled times.

Depending on your device and operating system, the App may request notification permission or additional alarm-related permissions.

These permissions are used to schedule and deliver reminders. You can manage notification and alarm permissions through your device settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

Reminder information, voice recordings, and imported audio files are stored locally on your device.

VoiceNudge does not require an account or operate a cloud account system for storing your reminder content.

Firebase Analytics processes technical and usage information as described in this Privacy Policy.

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Voice recordings, imported audio files, and reminder content are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers or Firebase Analytics.

VoiceNudge uses Google Firebase Analytics, a third-party service provided by Google, to collect and process app usage and technical information for analytics purposes.

Google may process information in accordance with its own privacy policies and terms.

The App may also rely on services provided by Apple or Google to support operating system functionality, such as notifications and alarm delivery.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Reminder information, voice recordings, and imported audio files remain on your device until you delete them or remove the App.

You can delete individual reminders and associated audio files through the App.

Uninstalling the App may remove locally stored application data. Information retained in device backups, if any, may be subject to your device backup settings.

Technical and usage information processed by Firebase Analytics is retained according to Google's applicable retention policies and the configuration of the Firebase project.

8. Children's Privacy

VoiceNudge is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request information about personal data processing.
  • Request access to personal information.
  • Request correction or deletion of personal information, where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Since reminder content and voice recordings are stored locally on your device, you can manage or delete this information directly within the App or through your device settings.

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us using the information provided below.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, third-party services, applicable laws, or our privacy practices.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

We encourage you to review this page periodically.
