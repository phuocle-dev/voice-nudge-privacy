Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

VoiceNudge respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Collect

VoiceNudge may collect limited technical information, such as:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version
  • Anonymous device identifier
  • Reminder notification events, such as scheduled or triggered notifications

Voice recordings created by you are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

How We Use Information

We use this information only to:

  • Provide and improve VoiceNudge
  • Monitor notification reliability
  • Identify and fix technical issues
  • Understand basic app usage and performance

We do not sell your personal information.

Data Storage

Reminder data and voice recordings are primarily stored on your device.

Limited technical and notification-tracking data may be stored securely using third-party services to help us monitor and improve the app.

Notifications

VoiceNudge requires notification permission to deliver reminders. Notification permissions can be managed through your device settings.

Your Privacy

VoiceNudge does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the updated version of this policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the contact information provided on the App Store.
